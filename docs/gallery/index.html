Send yours straight from here

Export your preset in Settings, Presets, Export, then drop the .awpreset here. The picture is drawn from the preset itself and everything else is read from the file, so the three boxes below are only for what it cannot know. A maintainer looks at it before it appears.

Add yours

A submission is one file. Everything a listing needs is already inside it and the picture is drawn from the preset itself, so the form above starts from what the package says and asks you only to confirm it.

  1. Export your preset Settings, Presets, Export. The file already carries the preset's name, version and preview.
  2. Choose it in the panel above Dropping it on the panel does the same thing. Choosing a file sends nothing: it waits while you fill in the rest.
  3. Check what the card will say All four boxes are filled in from the package the moment you choose it, credit included: that is the name the app recorded when you exported. Correct anything you want to read differently on the card. Only the name has to end up filled in.
  4. Press Publish The popup is rendered, and the preset waits for a maintainer to look at it. Nothing appears in the gallery until it is approved.
The details

The short rules

  • Your own work, with nothing in it you cannot redistribute.
  • Listed presets are published under CC BY 4.0.
  • 4 MB at most, and five submissions an hour.
  • A popup, not an advert, a system dialog or a link to somewhere.

Credit

The name on a card is the one you typed in the Credit box when you sent it. Leave it empty and the card simply carries none.

Steam Achievement Notifier themes

They are not listed here. SAN themes are .san files passed between users: there is no folder in the SAN repository, no wiki list and no feed to index, and the files belong to the people who made them, who have not licensed them for redistribution.

AW Next reads yours instead. Import a .san file in Settings, Presets, and it becomes an ordinary preset you can keep editing. Nothing in the theme file is executed, only pictures and audio come out of it, and whatever could not be converted is listed by name rather than dropped in silence.

What converts Steam Achievement Notifier

Converted a theme of yours? Export it as an .awpreset and submit that. It is then your preset, credited to you.
