    Add yours

    A submission is one file. Everything a listing needs is already inside it and the picture is rendered from the theme itself, so there is nothing to screenshot: the form above starts from what the package says and asks you only to confirm it.

    1. Export your theme Settings, Theme, Export. The file already carries the name, the version, the palette and any image you used.
    2. Choose it in the panel above Dropping it on the panel does the same thing. Choosing a file sends nothing: it waits while you fill in the rest.
    3. Check what the card will say All four boxes are filled in from the package the moment you choose it, credit included: that is the name the app recorded when you exported. Correct anything you want to read differently on the card. Only the name has to end up filled in.
    4. Press Publish The sample app is drawn with your theme and photographed, and the theme waits for a maintainer to look at it. Nothing appears in the gallery until it is approved.
    The details

    The short rules

    • Your own work, with nothing in it you cannot redistribute.
    • Listed themes are published under CC BY 4.0.
    • 8 MB at most, and five submissions an hour.
    • A theme, not an advert: no logos or text baked into a background image.

    Credit

    The name on a card is the one you typed in the Credit box when you sent it, or the author the package carries. Leave both empty and the card simply carries none.

    What is inside an .awtheme

    Colours, gradients, fits and effect settings, plus whatever background images the theme uses. That is the whole format: there is no stylesheet, no markup and no script anywhere in it, so an imported theme has nothing it could run and no way to reach the network. The app builds its own stylesheet from those values, exactly as it does for a theme you draw yourself.

    Which is also why the file never carries anything about the machine it came from: an image travels as bytes under a name built from the layer it belongs to, never as a path out of somebody's picture folder.
