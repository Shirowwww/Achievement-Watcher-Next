# Achievement Watcher Next 3.11.2

Collections, trophy mode, profile backup, a tidier Settings and a shorter first-run guide, plus fonts and logos in your own notification presets and a round of fixes.

## Highlights

- **Collections.** Group games into your own collections from a game's right-click menu, then filter the library to one with the button next to the installed-only toggle. Each has a name, a colour and an icon or picture, and the profile numbers follow the collection you are viewing.
- **Trophy mode.** Settings > General > Rarity display can grade every achievement gold, silver or bronze by its global unlock rate, in the game window, the overlay, the profile and the notification popups, with platinum for finished games.
- **Back up and restore your profile.** Settings > Advanced > Profile backup saves your settings, library choices, presets, themes, covers, restore points and playtime to one `.awbackup` file, for a reinstall or a new PC. Sign-ins are never saved.
- **Fonts and logos in the preset designer.** Pick a font file and a logo for your own notification presets; both travel inside the `.awpreset` package. The designer can also start from a bundled preset and layer your changes on top.
- **RUNE's Ubisoft Connect emulator** is found on its own, with live notifications.
- **A tidier Settings and a shorter first-run guide.** Sections, folded rarely used options, a "Found on this PC" report during setup, and a guide that can now really be skipped.
- **Keyboard and screen-reader use.** Focus rings everywhere, Tab, Enter and Space on the toolbar, tabs and lists, and Settings behaves as a proper dialog.
- **Counter achievements move in the library while you play**, not only at the next scan.
- **Fewer antivirus reactions.** Updates no longer rewrite the app's window files in `%APPDATA%`, which Kaspersky rolled back, and a scan starts PowerShell far less often.
- **More fixes:** the overlay no longer spoils hidden achievements, updates are refused when their signature cannot be checked, and the Steam account library no longer comes back with the Steam source set to Installed.

See the [full changelog](https://github.com/Shirowwww/Achievement-Watcher-Next/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#3112---2026-10-09) for the complete list.

## Install

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Achievement.Watcher.Setup.3.11.2.exe` | The installer. An installed copy also updates itself. |
| `Achievement.Watcher.Portable.3.11.2.zip` | The same build with no installer: extract it anywhere, it keeps its settings, caches and logs in a `data` folder beside the executable. |
| `.blockmap` and `latest.yml` | Used by automatic updates, nothing to download by hand. |
